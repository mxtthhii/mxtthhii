## ⚡ What I Build

I'm a CSE (AI & ML) undergraduate who builds end-to-end AI systems — from feature engineering and model design to evaluation and interpretability. My work spans machine learning, deep learning, NLP, computer vision, and reinforcement learning, and often combines several models into multimodal or ensemble pipelines. Explainability is part of the process: projects like the stroke detection system use SHAP, feature-importance analysis, and Grad-CAM to make multimodal predictions easier to interpret. I also work on research-oriented AI applications, from a research internship at IGCAR to a Formula 1 race strategy paper that is currently under review.

**Key Features**

**Technologies**

`Autoencoder` `GRU` `Random Forest`

**What I Implemented**

- Built the ensemble-based risk assessment system.
- Engineered lifestyle and clinical features for risk prediction.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Stroke Detection System</b></summary>

<br/>

**Overview**

A multimodal stroke detection pipeline that uses a dedicated model for each data type and explains its predictions.

**Key Features**

- MobileNetV2 for CT images, Random Forest for EEG signals, and XGBoost for clinical data
- Explainable AI with SHAP, feature-importance analysis, and Grad-CAM

**Technologies**

`MobileNetV2` `Random Forest` `XGBoost` `SHAP` `Grad-CAM`

**What I Implemented**

- Designed the multimodal detection pipeline.
- Applied explainability techniques to improve interpretability of the multimodal predictions.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>F1 Race Strategy Optimization</b></summary>

<br/>

**Overview**

A four-stage AI framework for optimizing Formula 1 race strategy. The accompanying research paper is under review.

**Key Features**

- Bi-LSTM with attention for lap-time prediction
- XGBoost for tyre-degradation modeling
- Stacking ensemble with SMOTE for pit-stop prediction
- PPO-based reinforcement learning agent that integrates the model predictions

**Technologies**

`Bi-LSTM` `Attention` `XGBoost` `Stacking Ensemble` `SMOTE` `PPO / Reinforcement Learning`

**What I Implemented**

- Designed the four-stage framework.
- Developed the PPO-based agent that combines model predictions to optimize overall race strategy.

</details>
